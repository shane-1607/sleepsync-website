YOUR DATA, YOUR SPACE

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what information SleepSync handles on its website and Android app. The website details below are based on the website code. The Android app’s data handling has not yet been verified against its source code or release package, so app-related statements are provisional.

Information saved by the website

The website does not require an account. If you use the sleep timer or log sleep hours, the date, duration, and timer start time are saved in local storage in your browser on your device. They are used to show your timer, sleep history, chart, and estimated score. The website code does not send these entries to a SleepSync server or sync them with the Android app.

The score is an estimate based on the sleep duration you enter. Sleep-stage labels are illustrative and are not measurements of your sleep or health.

Cookies, analytics, and technical information

The website code does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not include analytics, web beacons, or third-party tracking scripts. It uses browser local storage for the timer and sleep entries described above; local storage is different from cookies.

Your browser or website hosting provider may independently process standard technical information, such as IP addresses or request logs, when delivering and securing the website. We do not control the hosting provider’s collection, use, or retention practices. Check the hosting provider’s privacy information for details.

Audio and device features

Optional relaxation sounds on the website are generated in your browser when you choose to play them. The website does not record or transmit audio, use your microphone, or access health sensors.

Based on the developer’s current understanding, the Android app is not intended to collect or transmit personal information, and no permissions, advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are known to be used. The Android app has not been independently checked, so this information is not verified. Avoid entering sensitive personal or health information into the app until its behavior has been confirmed.

Third-party services and links

The website includes a download link to Google Drive. If you follow it, Google may process information under its own policies. SleepSync does not control Google Drive or other third-party websites and services. If you email the developer, your email address and message will be handled by your email provider and used to respond to you.

Storage, retention, and deletion

SleepSync does not receive website sleep entries, so it cannot retrieve or delete them for you. Entries remain in your browser’s local storage until removed or until your browser clears the site data. Use “Clear sleep history” on the Trends page to delete saved sleep entries. Stop a running timer to remove its saved start time, or clear this site’s local storage through your browser settings.

Local data may be lost if you clear browser data, use private browsing, change browsers, or change devices. Website hosting providers may keep technical logs under their own retention practices. The Android app’s storage, retention, and deletion behavior has not been verified; contact the developer with questions about the app.

Children’s privacy

SleepSync is a general-audience wellness tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Website sleep entries remain in the browser on the device. Take care when using a shared device and clear entries when appropriate. The Android app’s handling of children’s information has not been verified.

Security

Website entries are stored by your browser on your device and are not protected by a SleepSync account or server-side backup. Protect access to your device and clear locally stored entries if you no longer want them there. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Health disclaimer

SleepSync is for general informational and wellness purposes only. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat conditions, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Talk with a qualified healthcare professional about concerns with your sleep or health.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when SleepSync’s features or data practices change, including after the Android app is verified. The latest version and its update date will be shown on this page.

Product requirements document

SleepSync · Draft 1.0 · November 6, 2026

1. Product overview

SleepSync is a sleep-wellness companion intended to help users establish a bedtime routine, explore relaxation audio, learn basic sleep-hygiene practices, and review sleep-related information.

The supplied app screens show four main areas: Cycle, Relax, Guides, and Trends. A separate website presents the project and includes a browser-based sleep timer, manual sleep log, relaxation sounds, and guides.

2. Product goals

  • Give users a simple place to start and end a bedtime sleep-timer session.
  • Offer relaxation audio options for a user-controlled wind-down routine.
  • Present approachable educational guidance about sleep habits and sleep stages.
  • Let users enter sleep duration and review a history or trend summary.
  • Describe data handling clearly and avoid presenting estimates as clinical measurements.

3. Intended users

People looking for a lightweight sleep-wellness aid: users who want to time a bedtime routine, use calming audio, read sleep-hygiene tips, or keep a simple personal record. The app is not intended to diagnose or treat sleep disorders.

4. User-facing feature requirements

  • Cycle: Present a sleep timer and controls to begin and end a session. The source screenshots show elapsed time and sleep-stage labels; whether stage values are calculated, manually entered, or sensor-derived is unconfirmed.
  • Relax: Present selectable relaxation-audio options and a way to stop playback. The original app's audio assets, playback implementation, and offline behavior are unconfirmed.
  • Guides: Provide readable sleep-hygiene guidance, such as a regular sleep schedule, a restful environment, and winding down before bed.
  • Trends: Allow sleep duration to be logged and show a sleep score or summary. The scoring formula and the persistence behavior of the original Android app are unconfirmed.
  • Privacy: Explain what information is collected, where it is stored, whether it is shared, and how the user can delete it. Confirm the Android app's behavior before making app-specific privacy claims.

5. Scope and limitations

Heart-rate stability appears in the supplied promotional artwork, but no heart-rate sensor integration or measurement method has been confirmed. Do not promise heart-rate monitoring or automatic sleep-stage detection without a verified implementation and appropriate testing.

Sleep-cycle duration and stage descriptions are educational estimates unless confirmed otherwise. SleepSync is a general wellness project, not a medical device or substitute for professional care.

6. Acceptance criteria

  • Users can identify and navigate the Cycle, Relax, Guides, and Trends areas.
  • Timer controls give clear feedback about whether a session is running.
  • Sleep-duration entries and summaries are understandable and avoid implying clinical accuracy.
  • Audio starts only after a user action and can be stopped.
  • Privacy and health limitations are easy to find and written in plain language.
  • The experience is usable on common mobile screen sizes and with keyboard navigation.

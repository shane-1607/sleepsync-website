Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

SleepSync is a simple sleep-wellness website. This policy explains what information it handles and how you can manage it.

Information you enter

If you use the sleep timer or log sleep hours, those entries are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device. They are used to show your timer, sleep history, chart, and estimated score. SleepSync does not send those entries to a server.

The sleep score is a simple estimate based only on the hours you enter. Timer stage amounts are illustrative estimates, not measurements of your sleep or health.

Audio and device features

Optional relaxation sounds are generated in your browser when you choose to play them. SleepSync does not record or transmit audio, use your microphone, or access health sensors.

Cookies, analytics, and sharing

This site does not use advertising cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking. It does not sell, rent, or share your sleep entries. Your browser or hosting provider may independently process standard technical information needed to deliver a webpage; check their policies for details.

Your choices and deleting entries

You can remove your sleep entries using “Clear sleep history” on the Trends page, or clear this site’s local storage through your browser settings. Clearing browser data also removes locally saved entries. Private browsing settings or device changes may affect whether local data persists.

Children’s privacy

SleepSync is a general-audience wellness tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because entries stay in local browser storage, please use a shared device thoughtfully and clear entries when appropriate.

Health disclaimer

SleepSync is for general informational and wellness purposes only. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat conditions, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Talk with a qualified healthcare professional about concerns with your sleep or health.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the site’s features change. The latest version and its update date will be shown on this page. If you have a question about this policy, contact the person or organization that provided you with this SleepSync website.
